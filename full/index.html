Studio 001 — iv. principles

Four principles we work from.

Slow practice, fewer rooms, deeper attention — the only kind of scale we are interested in.

01.

Air, then floor plan.

Every restructure begins with a three-day climate study — light, CO₂, humidity, ambient noise. The brief follows the reading, not the other way around.

02.

Plants as infrastructure.

Planting plans before furniture plans. Every room ships with a care calendar and six months of post-handover work, on us.

03.

Quiet is a material.

Lime ceilings, oak slats, planting walls, wool floors. Acoustics tuned by source-test, not by vibe. The cheapest way to feel expensive is to be quiet.

04.

Four rooms a quarter.

Slower work, fewer projects, deeper attention. The team stays small — one botanist, two interior architects, an acoustician we trust.

vi. — the founder

The studio is run, quietly, by Angelina.

Trained as an interior architect in Milan and Delhi, Angelina opened the studio in 2022 with a single brief from a friend — restructure my office before I lose the team to the air.

Twelve rooms later, the practice still works the same way: a small team, four projects a quarter, every room grown rather than drawn. She is in the studio four days a week and on a floor the other three.

Angelina ~Founder & principal · Studio 001
