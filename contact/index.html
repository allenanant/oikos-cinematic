Tell us about the room, and we will read it back to you.

Most projects start the same way: someone walks their office and senses it is working against the people in it. If that sounds familiar, write to us. There is no brief to fill in yet. A few honest lines about the space is enough to begin.

A few lines is plenty. The more you can say about how the room actually feels to sit in, the more useful our first reply will be.

What happens next
01

You write, we read

Send the note and it reaches the studio directly. Within two working days you will hear back from a person, not an autoresponder, with a first reading of what you have described and a few questions worth answering.

02

We come and stand in it

If it feels right on both sides, we arrange a visit. We spend an hour or two in the space at the time of day it is hardest to be in, read the light, the air, the noise and the materials, and tell you plainly what the room is asking for.

03

You get something to hold

You receive a written reading and a ranked set of moves, with a sense of cost and sequence. Some offices need a full restructure. Many need three or four corrections. We will tell you which one you are, even when it is the smaller answer.
