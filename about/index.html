About · the studio

We restructure offices into rooms that breathe.

i. · the name
οἶκοςGreek for home.

The root, also, of both ecology and economy. The way a household is kept, and the world that keeps it.

Your workplace is your other home. So Oikos started with a simple conviction: it ought to be kept with the same care.

ii.

How a room is changed.

Light, air, quiet · three working systems
01Daylight-led restructure

Light, brought to the middle.

Pull light into the deepest, most dead centre of your space, through the core, not just the windows.

02Planting as a working system

Fresh air you actually feel.

We treat planting as air infrastructure, not decoration. A living system measured for what it puts back into the room.

03Acoustics as material

Quiet, built in.

We build quiet from the materials up, each design tuned by measurement. The easiest way to feel expensive is to be quiet.

iii. · our expertise

Sustainability, made approachable.

With Oikos you benefit from deep expertise in sustainability consultation. We combine environmental knowledge with a human-centred approach, so every recommendation is actionable, beautiful, and aligned with your values, so sustainability sits naturally in the way you work, with lasting impact for your team and the planet.

Empower your business with sustainable practice.

Share the details of your project with us, and take the first step towards a greener, more responsible future.
